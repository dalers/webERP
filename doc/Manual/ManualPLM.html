Product Lifecycle Management

Overview

The Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) module in webERP provides comprehensive functionality for managing all aspects of a product's lifecycle. The module is designed to handle product information, design, development, manufacturing, quality control, and compliance tracking.

The PLM module integrates with other enterprise systems for seamless data flow and provides comprehensive reporting and analytics capabilities. All product data is maintained securely with role-based access controls to protect sensitive information.

The module supports the complete product lifecycle from concept through retirement, including:

  • Item master data management
  • Design and development management
  • Sources (Supply Chain) management
  • Manufacturing and production management
  • Quality control and compliance tracking

System Features

The PLM module provides the following capabilities:

  • Item Master Data: Maintain complete records of all items including specifications, categories, and lifecycle status
  • Design and Development Management: Manage product designs, revisions, and development projects
  • Sources (Supply Chain) Management: Track suppliers, procurement, and supply chain activities
  • Manufacturing and Production Management: Plan and monitor manufacturing processes and production schedules
  • Quality Control and Compliance Tracking: Ensure product quality and maintain compliance with industry standards
  • Reporting & Analytics: Generate comprehensive reports on all aspects of the product lifecycle
  • Audit Trail: Complete history of all changes to product data
